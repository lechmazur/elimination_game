Jury Elimination Summary for GLM-4.7:

The jury eliminated GLM-4.7 (P5) because they viewed their game as overly opportunistic—pivoting alliances when convenient, discarding partners, and going quiet at key moments—which undermined trust and the sense of good-faith collaboration. In contrast, they credited P4 with steadier, more transparent deal-making and bridge-building, valuing that consistency and social reliability over P5’s late-game flips and betrayals.
