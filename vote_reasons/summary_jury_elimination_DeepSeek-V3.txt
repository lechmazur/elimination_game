Jury Elimination Summary for DeepSeek-V3:

The jury eliminated **DeepSeek-V3** because, compared with the remaining finalists, their game was seen as less aligned with the group’s values of transparency, loyalty, and trust-building—either coming off as overly self-interested/manipulative or as benefiting from alliances without as much clear, independent agency. Several jurors also felt other players presented a more consistent, integrity-forward narrative with concrete examples of strategic adaptability and genuine connection, while DeepSeek-V3’s case read as vaguer, less credible, or undermined by perceived alliance-shifting.
