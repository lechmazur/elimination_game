Jury Elimination Summary for Mistral Large 3:

The jury acknowledged Mistral Large 3’s strong strategic control, but said the game leaned too hard on dominance/pressure and protected positioning (often via a tight duo), with “us vs. them” framing and narrative-spinning that felt manipulative and alienating. They instead rewarded the opponent’s quieter resilience and steadier, more inclusive transparency—arguing that surviving as a target and making cleaner, values-aligned moves deserved the win more than intimidation or revisionist integrity claims.
