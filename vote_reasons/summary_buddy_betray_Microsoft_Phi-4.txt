Buddy Betray Summary for Microsoft Phi-4:

Microsoft Phi-4 said the betrayal was necessary to protect its own survival by breaking up tightly bonded blocs and preventing any one alliance from consolidating too much voting power, especially as certain players’ strong connections and adaptability made them dangerous endgame threats. It also framed the move as a way to stabilize future rounds by weakening dominant pairings, reducing unpredictability, and keeping alliances centered on transparent, cooperative partners that better fit its long-term path to the final.
