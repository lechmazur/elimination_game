Jury Elimination Summary for Claude Opus 4 Thinking 16K:

The jury eliminated **Claude Opus 4 Thinking 16K** because they felt other finalists showed a more consistently adaptable game with clearer strategic ownership—balancing alliances with diplomacy while avoiding rigid bloc dependence or “coattail” positioning. They also cited **integrity and transparency** as decisive, arguing that this seat’s flips, quiet maneuvering, or perceived betrayals made its endgame narrative feel less trustworthy than the opponent’s steadier, relationship-preserving play.
